Holding an Ace (Blackjack Terms)In blackjack, an Ace is unique because it can be worth 1 or 11 points.Soft Hand / Soft Total: Any hand that contains an Ace where it is currently counted as 11 without the total exceeding 21.Natural / Snapper: A "natural" blackjack is an Ace and a 10-value card (10, J, Q, K) dealt as the first two cards.Soft 17: A specific hand (Ace-6) that is famous because many casino rules require the dealer to either hit or stand on it.Ace-Rich / Ace-Poor: Terms used by card counters to describe whether the remaining deck has a high or low density of Aces.Poker-Style Nicknames (Applied to Aces)If you are looking for creative nicknames similar to "pocket rockets," these are common for any Ace-based starting hand:Bullets: Refers to the "A" looking like a bullet tip.American Airlines: Based on the "AA" initials.Rocket Queen: Specifically for an Ace and a Queen (A-Q).Snake Eyes: Borrowed from craps, often used when someone is dealt two Aces (which you would usually split in blackjack).Batteries: Because "AA" batteries "charge up" the hand.Teepees / Needles: Visual nicknames for the sharp points of the letter "A".Hand-Specific NicknamesBlackjack / 21: The ultimate two-card hand.Apple Jacks / Ajax: Nicknames for an Ace-Jack (A-J) hand.Little Slick: A nickname for Ace-Queen (A-Q), a step down from "Big Slick" (A-K).Five-Card Charlie: A hand where you draw five cards without busting, which sometimes wins automatically depending on house rules.
